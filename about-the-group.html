Who we are

One group, two markets — and no reason to hide either one.

A lot of security vendors would rather you didn't ask where they're really based. We'd rather you knew, because it's the more useful part of the story.

Brazil — Group HQ

GLCTech

GLCTech was founded in São Paulo and has spent 9+ years building managed monitoring, security and backup practices — Zabbix-based infrastructure monitoring, Kaspersky-backed endpoint security, and Veeam-based backup — for SMEs and managed service clients across Brazil.

This is where the engineering depth of the group comes from: the 24/7 monitoring operation, the automation, the incident response discipline that GLCTech Sec applies to UK clients.

São Paulo, Brazil Founding team, core engineering & monitoring operations
United Kingdom

GLCTech Sec

GLCTech Sec is the group's international division, set up to serve UK SMEs in regulated or client-sensitive sectors — accountancy, legal, financial and professional services.

Same platform, same engineering team behind it, with a UK-specific compliance layer: UK GDPR handling, ICO-aligned breach reporting, DPAs, and vendor selection built around what UK procurement actually asks for.

Salford, Greater Manchester, United Kingdom UK client-facing operations & compliance

Why tell you this at all?

Because the alternative — presenting GLCTech Sec as if it were a standalone UK company with no history — doesn't survive a second Google search, and it isn't the story we want to tell anyway. Nine years of operational track record is an asset. We'd rather lead with it and be specific about how it applies to you than hope nobody asks.

What stays the same across both markets

  • The same core disciplines: infrastructure monitoring, managed security, and backup and continuity.
  • The same engineering team and 24/7 monitoring operation behind the alerts you see.
  • A vendor-agnostic approach to security tooling — the platform is chosen to fit you, not the other way round. See our Trust & Compliance Centre.

What's different for UK clients

  • Pricing in GBP, contracts and data processing agreements under UK GDPR.
  • Incident response and breach notification aligned to the ICO's 72-hour expectation.
  • Security vendor shortlist and documentation built around UK procurement and insurer questionnaires.
  • A UK point of contact for account management, based in Greater Manchester.

Where we are honest about being early

Our UK client base is new — most of our track record so far is Brazilian, and our testimonials on the homepage say so. We're building our first UK case studies now. If that matters to your decision, the fastest way to test us is a no-obligation Security Gap Assessment rather than taking our word for it.
